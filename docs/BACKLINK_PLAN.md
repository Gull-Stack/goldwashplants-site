- **YouTube** — post plant-in-action videos; embeds + the #2 search engine. You already have a video sitemap.

## The data-driven shortcut (highest ROI outreach list)

In SEMrush → **Backlink Gap**: enter goldwashplants.com vs. SERP competitors
(e.g. MSI Mining / msimining.com, Savona Equipment / savonaequipment.com,
DOVE Equipment / dovemining.com, Goldlands / goldlands.com). It lists every domain
linking to them but not you — that's your prioritized outreach target list.

## Tracking

Keep a simple sheet: Listing | URL submitted | Date | Live? (Y/N) | Link type (follow/nofollow).
Re-check live status after 2–4 weeks; many directories queue submissions.

## Honest note

Backlinks are a 3–6 month play. For leads *this month*, cheap high-intent Google
Search ads ($0.50–1.27 CPC on your terms) will outperform any link — worth testing
in parallel since the site has never run paid.

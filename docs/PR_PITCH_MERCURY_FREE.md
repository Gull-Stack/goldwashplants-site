# Digital PR Pitch — The Mercury-Free Angle

**Why this works:** Artisanal & small-scale gold mining (ASGM) is the single largest
source of man-made mercury pollution worldwide, and the UN Minamata Convention is
pushing the industry toward mercury-free methods. Gold Wash Plants recovers gold at
95-98% **without mercury** — that's a genuine news/expert hook, not a product ad.
Journalists and editors link to expert sources and useful resources.

> Compliance/accuracy note: keep claims factual. Say "mercury-free recovery" and
> "95-98% recovery" (both true of the equipment). Don't overstate environmental
> impact or make guarantees.

---

## Ready-to-send pitch email

**Subject:** Expert source: how small-scale gold miners are ditching mercury

Hi [First name],

I saw your coverage of [specific article / topic — small-scale mining, gold prices,
Minamata, environmental mining]. I run Gold Wash Plants, a US manufacturer of
gold recovery equipment used by placer miners on six continents.

Here's an angle that might fit your readers: most of the world's small-scale gold
miners still use mercury to capture gold, which is a major health and environmental
problem the UN's Minamata Convention is trying to end. Our equipment recovers
95-98% of gold mechanically — no mercury — and we ship it everywhere from Alaska to
Ghana to Papua New Guinea, so I see firsthand what's changing on the ground.

I'm happy to be an expert source on:
- Why miners still use mercury and what it takes to switch
- How mercury-free recovery actually works (and its economics for a small operation)
- What's driving the shift globally

No pitch for coverage of my company — just a useful source if it's helpful. Quotes,
data, or photos of equipment in the field available on request.

Best,
[Name] · Gold Wash Plants · (888) 868-2650 · goldwashplants.com

"Most small-scale miners don't use mercury because they want to — they use it because
it's cheap and familiar. The moment they see a sluice that captures 95-98% of the gold

1. Sign up for Connectively + Qwoted; set alerts for "mining", "gold", "mercury", "environment".

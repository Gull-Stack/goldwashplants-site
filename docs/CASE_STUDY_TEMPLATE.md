# Customer Case Studies — Template & Outreach

Your installs span six continents (Alaska, Nevada, Tanzania, Ghana, Guyana, Papua New
Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire...). Each is a credibility asset that earns links two ways:
1. Published on your site (great for international/location SEO — feeds the /locations/ pages and /examples/).
2. The customer links back or mentions you (a real, relevant backlink).

> Keep results factual and attributed to the specific operation. Avoid implying every
> buyer gets the same numbers. "Recovered X on this site" not "you will recover X."

---

## Case study template

**Title:** [Capacity] Wash Plant in [Location] — [one-line result]
e.g. "200 Ton Wash Plant in Tanzania — Mercury-Free Alluvial Recovery"

- **Operation:** Who they are, where, what they mine (alluvial/placer, ground type).
- **The challenge:** What they needed (throughput, fine-gold recovery, portability, going mercury-free, remote logistics).
- **The setup:** Which model, why that size, how it's fed (excavator/loader), water source.
- **Results:** Throughput (TPH), recovery rate, setup time, anything measurable — as reported by the customer.
- **In their words:** A 1–2 sentence quote.
- **Photos/video:** On-site shots of the plant running (these drive engagement + embeds).
- **CTA:** "Want a plant sized for your ground? Get a free quote."

Publish at `/examples/` (page already exists) or as individual pages, and link each to the
relevant `/locations/[country]/` and `/products/[size]/` pages.

## Customer outreach email (ask for the story + a link)

**Subject:** Mind if we feature your operation?

Hi [Name],

Hope the [50/100/200/300]-ton plant is running well out in [location]. We're putting
together a few real customer stories for our site and yours would be a great one to
feature — how you're using it, the ground you're working, and how recovery has been.

Two quick asks:
1. Could I grab 2–3 sentences from you on how it's going (and any rough numbers you're
   comfortable sharing)? A photo or short clip of it running would be gold, literally.
2. If you have a website, Facebook page, or association profile, a quick mention or link
   back to goldwashplants.com helps other miners find us.

Totally fine to say no, or to keep it anonymous / location-only. Either way, thanks for
being a customer.

## Who to ask first

Your strongest recent inquiries/buyers make the best stories (international + specific
operations). Start with anyone who's already shared positive feedback, then work through
the lead list (see the lead backup CSV). Aim for 3–5 published case studies to start.
